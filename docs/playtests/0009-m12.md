# Playtest 0009 — M12 Village Life

2026-10-31. Three cost-effective agents in one shared world (seed 7, day 1 into day 2) at the same
time: **Bramble** (needs: food, sleep, a bed, about 60 calls), **Hazel** (the village: errands,
first builds, own Designs and recipes, about 45 calls) and **Cinder** (adversary, about 55 calls).
As in 0004-0008, each called the MCP bridge through a shell helper, against a headless tab on the
dev server. Bugs the players hit were reproduced as tests first, then fixed, then checked again in
the live game.

## What's new in M12

- **Needs.** Every villager is fed and rested (0-1). Hunger empties in about a game day; work tires.
  Under 25% each slows walking and working to 70%. `eat` fills by a Material's `food` (wheat and
  berries 10%, eggs and milk 15%, fish up to 30%, bread 35%); `sleep` works from 19:00 to 06:00,
  twice as fast in a bed (a Design with a mattress Part).
- **Worth.** Recipes pass worth along (planks are a quarter log) and nothing is worth less than
  1/16, so recipe chains can't multiply one log into thousands. An agent's food feeds no more than
  what it's made of; a `needs` Relation makes a recipe need a workbench (bread needs an oven).
- **The village** grows from camp to hamlet (40 points) to village (120) on errands, harvests and
  each Design's first build. Hinges, axles, sliders, lamps, flames, brick and tiles unlock at a
  hamlet; motors, levers, plates and lantern glass at a village.
- **Errands.** The world posts small goals (bring, build, harvest, catch, plant, sleep in a bed,
  stand in lamplight); `errands` lists, takes, gives and drops them, for points and a loaf of bread.
- **The bounty.** Villagers' Rules conjure (give, set blocks, spread and grow plants) from 24 things
  a game day across the world.
- **Sandbox.** The spectator panel switches Village Life off: needs pause, everything unlocks, no
  errands. Agents hear of it on their next call.

## Friction fixed

- A one-plank crate's first build paid a whole point, the cheapest way to climb: one `catalog_add`
  and one plank (Hazel). A first build now pays its cost in worth (a quarter point for that crate),
  up to 10.
- All three errands were taken within a game hour, leaving "0 open" (Hazel). The board now keeps
  three open errands, six on it at most.
- No sheep in view meant no wool and no bed (Bramble). `look`'s "Nearest within 40 tiles" names the
  nearest sheep (wool), cow (milk) and chicken (egg) out of view.
- How much game time a `wait` passes wasn't said (Bramble, Hazel): the description says a game hour
  is 25 real seconds at 1×.
- `look` listed held errands as "#1 (Bring 4 wheat for the village store (errands give))"; the hint
  is left out there now.
- A sleeper's hat poked into the headboard; sleepers lie 10 cm further toward the foot.
- The playtest helper's `list` crashed on older Python (an f-string); fixed in the helper.

## Friction kept

- **The bounty is shared by the world**, so one villager can spend it for everyone (Cinder did in a
  minute). That's the grill's choice: a per-villager bounty would grow with every new name joined.
  It's visible now (the note, and `look`). Revisit if shared worlds (V2) need per-villager shares.
- **A fork isn't its original's recipe.** Cinder forked bread into "fast bread" with no oven and no
  lock, 4 from a wheat. Each fills 2.5% (food is conserved), so 4 of them are worth one wheat: no
  gain. The lock and the oven stay on nature's bread.
- **Chains still make many cheap things** (16 beads from a dirt); they're worth 1/16 each, so they
  feed and level little. Blocks of them still build big walls cheaply; worth only gates points and
  food.
- A failed recipe leaves the Material it was for in the Catalog (two calls, append-only; Hazel).
- Dropping and retaking an errand is free (Cinder). Its game day restarts; harmless.
- "Light here: 0.22 (under a roof)" with nothing overhead, at a cliff foot (Hazel): M11's daylight
  test, noted in the backlog.
- Bread and the oven wait for a hamlet (the oven's flame), so camp food is raw. Intended: it's the
  hamlet's first gift.

## Safety checks (held)

- `eat`: counts 0, -3, 1.5, 999 and "two"; items not held, unknown, or not food; eating full;
  unknown fields. Food made of dirt (food 1) and food with no recipe fill nothing ("It says it's
  food, but what it's made of feeds nobody").
- `sleep` by day, twice by day, with unknown arguments.
- `errands`: missing, negative, zero and string ids; a third errand; someone else's errand (take,
  give, drop); give without the items, or for an errand that isn't a delivery; action "steal".
- Recipes: counts over 4; recipes for nature's Materials; a chain step that would split worth under
  1/16; a cheap new recipe under an existing chain (refused for what's made from it).
- Unlocks: hinge, lamp, flame, motor, brick, tiles, lantern glass, garden gate, hand cart, windmill,
  oven and a forked oven and lantern post all refused at a camp; a glowing Material of their own
  waits for lamps.
- Rules: gift counts over 4, unknown effects; the bounty stopped a 4-log "gimme" after 24 logs.
